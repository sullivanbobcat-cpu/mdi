How these numbers are built

Inventory figures are EIA's weekly estimates: commercial crude oil stocks exclude the Strategic Petroleum Reserve, and natural gas storage is working gas in the Lower 48. The shaded band is the highest and lowest reading for each ISO week across the five prior calendar years, and the dashed line is their average.

Spot prices are the daily series published in EIA's open data API. EIA sources these from third-party price reporters, and other EIA products can show slightly different values for the same day, so treat them as reference prices rather than settlement prices. Physical spot prices can also sit well above or below the front-month futures prices quoted in the news, especially during supply disruptions.

Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration open data, including the Weekly Petroleum Status Report and the Weekly Natural Gas Storage Report.
