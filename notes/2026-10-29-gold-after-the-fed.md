## What moved

- **The Fed held at 3.75%–4.00% in a 10–2 vote** on October 28. Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack dissented in favor of a quarter-point hike. Chair Kevin Warsh called growth strong and inflation too high, gave no guidance on the next move, and said the Fed will likely cut its rate decisions from eight to six a year.
- **Spot gold fell as much as 1.3% to near $4,015** after the decision, reversing three days of gains as long-end Treasury yields rose.
- **Thursday's data added pressure.** September headline PCE rose 0.5% on the month, lifting the annual rate to 3.6% from 3.4%. Core PCE held at 3.1%. Q3 GDP came in at 3.4%, and weekly jobless claims were 194,000. Spot gold sat near $4,022 on Thursday morning, down about 1.1%, after a $4,015–$4,070 range.
- **Context:** gold is roughly 28% below its January 2026 record and heading for a second straight monthly loss, down about 2.5% in October.

## Why

A hold with two hike dissents and reaccelerating headline inflation reads as a hawkish pause. A non-yielding asset struggles when real yields stay firm and a December hike stays live. Before the meeting, markets priced roughly 81% odds of at least one hike by December 9, and this week's data didn't argue against that.

The floor around $4,000 has held on central bank buying and safe-haven demand. Goldman Sachs trimmed its year-end 2026 fair value to $4,650 from $4,900, still well above spot.

## What to watch

| Date | Event | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Tue Nov 3 | Midterms; JOLTS | Results arrive overnight |
| Fri Nov 6 | October jobs report | First labor read after the hold |
| Tue Nov 10 | October CPI | Last inflation print that counts before December |
| Wed Dec 9 | FOMC + dot plot | Hike or no hike |

**The election** is likely the smallest of these. Forecasters see Democrats favored to take the House, so divided government is largely priced. The channel to gold is fiscal: bigger-deficit outcomes can lift yields while feeding longer-run dollar concerns. Gold rose in the month after each of the last five midterms, but that's five data points, and none came with a Fed weighing hikes.

**CPI on November 10** matters most. A soft core reading would ease December hike odds and is the clearest path back above the ~$4,070 area where gold stalled this week. A hot reading, or a strong jobs report on the 6th, would put the $4,000 level under more pressure.
