    How these numbers are built

    Barge freight on the inland waterways is quoted as a percent of the 1976 Waterways Freight Bureau tariff. Each origin has its own benchmark, so a 300% rate at Twin Cities costs more than a 300% rate at St. Louis. This page converts every quote to dollars per ton: percent of tariff ÷ 100 × the origin's benchmark.

    The seasonal average uses the five prior calendar years, matched by ISO week. Lock tonnage sums all grains reported for each lock and week.
